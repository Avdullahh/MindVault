## Description

MindVault is a second-brain app for turning loose ideas into connected plans.

Capture thoughts quickly, organize them into ideas, and link them to the goals, tasks, and projects they belong to. MindVault is built for people who want more than a generic notes app, but do not want a heavy system that gets in the way of thinking.

Use MindVault to keep track of what matters, connect related work, and move from capture to action with a calm, focused workspace. The app is designed to support you deeply analyze your projects or quickly capture an idea on the go.

Core features include idea capture, goal tracking, project organization, task planning, and cross-linking between the things you are thinking about and the things you are doing.

AI-assisted features are optional and user-triggered. They are not required to use MindVault, and they never run automatically.
